Assignment Instructions 
You have been asked to produce a cloud-based web application for the University of Winchester, which records the registered location of their fire wardens on campus. The web-application should be hosted using Microsoft Azure and is intended to be accessed via PCs and laptops.

Currently, the University has approximately 70 staff who voluntarily carry-out fire warden duties alongside their contractual duties. A fire warden is responsible for assisting in the ongoing management of fire safety and ensuring the safety of people during a fire evacuation. Due to the University having many buildings across three campuses and an established remote working policy, identifying the location of fire wardens each day is challenging. This makes it difficult to understand their availability should an evacuation be required.

 

A list of required functionalities is set out below:

The application should allow fire wardens to log their working location at the beginning of each day and make amendments during the day if required. Further to this, the following functionality is required:

Record the fire warden’s unique staff number
Record the fire warden’s first and surname
Record the fire warden’s working location*
Record the date and time of the entry
Amend recorded details
Delete recorded details
Provide a dashboard for the Health and Safety team to view the recorded locations of all fire warden staff
At this stage, the University views your cloud-based web application as a prototype. Implementation with the University mobile application, or deployment as a standalone mobile application, may occur at a later date.

*A list of locations/buildings has been provided later in this brief, for this purpose

Development Portfolio

You will work individually on producing a cloud-based web application, and then submit your work individually as a Development Portfolio.

The Development Portfolio must include a written report as well as copies of the development files. These should be submitted together as a .zip file.

Your written report must be saved as a .doc OR .docx file, and have the following sections:

Executive Summary (150 words)
In this section, you should introduce your portfolio and define your client’s requirements. You should also discuss the opportunities and challenges they present.

System design and discussion (750 words plus one or two diagrams)
Here, you should present a discussion of the importance of cloud system design, considering the complexities of:
Scalability
Vendor lock-in and portability
Virtualisation
Cost control
Reusability
Availability
Note: You should provide one or two diagrams of the main elements of your full-stack solution to show how the different elements come together in your design.


Implementation Details (600 words plus one key code snippet to be commented on)
Explain how your final cloud-based web application is suitable as a cloud deployment. Detail some of the key development processes and considerations. You should address the following aspects:
Your use of programming languages/libraries
Your approach to working with a Microsoft Azure database solution
Any styling considerations (if applicable)
Note: You should provide code snippets (maximum of three), where appropriate, to help illustrate your points.


Reflection (500 words)
Provide a critical reflection upon your creative and learning processes and approaches. Your may wish to address the following questions:
What did you learn from the process of developing your cloud-based web application?
What further improvements and/or processes might be required before final deployment?
What pain points might there be when producing a mobile version of your cloud-based web application?
What challenges did you come across during the development process, and how did you overcome them? Were there any ‘light bulb’ moments?
What aspects of the development process did you find easy? Did you find opportunity to challenge yourself further in this area?
Which areas of your knowledge or skillset do you feel you need to develop further?
